reaction: The commenter reports their own feeling or experience of the movie — what they loved, laughed at, gasped at, or how their theater responded — without explaining why the film works or what it means.
analysis: The commenter makes a claim about a character, theme, or filmmaking choice and supports it by pointing to something specific inside the film — a scene, a line, a shot, or a plot event.
trivia: The comment's main content is information from outside the movie itself — behind-the-scenes facts, cast and crew background, interviews, real-life events, or other books, shows, and films.
